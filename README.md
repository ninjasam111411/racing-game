# Kart Rush GP

A racing game you play right in your web browser. Nothing to download or install.

## ▶ Play

**https://ninjasam111411.github.io/racing-game/**

Works best on a computer with a keyboard, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

## Game modes

- **Grand Prix**: a driving sim. Manage tyre wear and fuel, time your pit stop, and beat a full field of rivals.
- **Arcade**: item boxes, drift boosts and banana peels.
- **Multiplayer**: race your friends online with a room code.
- **Garage**: spend your race winnings on engine, aero, grip, brakes and a faster pit crew, and pick your car's paint.

## Controls

### Grand Prix
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → | Steer |
| ↑ | Throttle |
| ↓ | Brake (reverse when stopped) |
| Shift | Handbrake |
| E / Q | Shift up / down (manual gears) |
| B | Box this lap (call a pit stop) |
| T | Choose tyres for the stop |
| F | Choose fuel to add |
| R | Back on track (+5 s penalty) |
| Tab (hold) | Standings |
| C | Driver's seat / chase camera |
| P | Pause |
| M | Sound on / off |

### Arcade
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → | Steer |
| ↑ / ↓ | Gas / brake |
| Shift | Drift: hold in a turn, let go for a boost |
| E | Use item |
| R | Back on track |
| C | Camera |
| P | Pause |

## Racing tips

- **Pit stops:** press **B** when your tyres wear down, then follow the yellow arrows to your box. With driver aids
  on **Full** or **Normal**, the car drives itself down the pit lane and into the box.
- **Fuel weighs a lot:** a full tank makes the car slower to accelerate and brake. Less fuel means more speed, but more stops.
- **Spinning?** Steer into the slide to catch it. The on-screen arrow shows which way.
- **Driver aids:** choose Full, Normal or Pro in the Grand Prix setup. Pro turns off traction control, ABS and stability help.
- **Graphics:** if the game stutters, set **Graphics** on the start screen to **High** or **Low**. **Ultra** looks best on a fast computer.

## Multiplayer

1. Click **Multiplayer** and type your name.
2. **To host:** click **Host a race**. Share the 6-letter room code, or click **Copy invite link** and send it.
3. **To join:** type the room code and click **Join**, or just open the invite link a friend sent you.
4. The host chooses the number of laps and presses **Start race**.

- Up to 8 players. Empty places on the grid are filled with AI cars.
- Multiplayer races are Grand Prix races. You race your own car with your own paint, upgrades and driver aids.
- If someone can't connect, try a different network (for example a phone hotspot). Some school and office networks block it.

## Your progress

Credits, upgrades and settings are saved in your own browser. Clearing your browser's site data resets them.
